TermsGuard

AI-powered analysis for Terms of Service, Privacy Policies, and user agreements.

Download Extension (.zip)

How to install (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Download the latest TermsGuard zip from the link above.
  2. Unzip the file to a folder on your computer.
  3. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions.
  4. Enable “Developer mode” (top right toggle).
  5. Click “Load unpacked” and select the unzipped TermsGuard folder.

How to use

  1. Open any Terms & Conditions or Privacy Policy page.
  2. Click the TermsGuard icon in your browser toolbar.
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions to summarize or highlight risk clauses.
